Dear Mitchelle's Mother

This is your beautiful daughter, Mitchelle Terry

The amazing woman I'm asking to know better

I hope this letter finds you well. My name is Stephen, and I come to you with respect, sincerity, and a full heart.

I want to tell you how Mitchelle and I met. She is a friend of my sister, and they studied together at Kiriri Women's University of Science and Technology. One day my sister brought her over to my place, and from the moment I saw her, something clicked. She wasn't just my sister's friend anymore. She became someone I wanted to know, to laugh with, and to build something real with.

I'm a Luo from Siaya County, raised by both my mother and father. Family is everything to me. My father passed away on June 2nd, 2024, and that loss reshaped how I see love and time. I have two elder brothers who are already married, and two sisters, so it's just me left at home, which means when I commit to someone, I commit fully. Mitchelle will be my priority, and I will protect what we build.

By God's grace, my mother has already met Mitchelle, and she blessed us to live together in peace. That blessing means the world to me, because a mother's approval is not something I take lightly.
